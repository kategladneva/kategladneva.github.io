Loyalty program

Previous loyalty program

The previous loyalty program consisted of six discount tiers:
0% – 5% – 10% – 15% – 20% – 25%
To move up to the next tier, users had to accumulate a certain number of points (1 RUB = 1 point) and maintain a required purchase completion rate

User problems Based on user research

  • No incentive for new users. When creating an account, new users started with a 0% discount. This made the loyalty program feel unappealing and discouraged them from placing their first order.
  • Confusing points system. Existing users didn’t understand how the points system worked and assumed they could redeem points for purchases, as they could in other loyalty programs.
  • Complex rules. Overall, users found the program’s rules and requirements difficult to understand.

Business challenges

  • High discount costs. We were offering discounts that were too generous, negatively impacting profitability.
  • New user churn. The existing loyalty model contributed to high churn among new users.
  • Low perceived value. Since Lamoda regularly offered discounts and promotions, users didn’t see much additional value in the loyalty program and had little motivation to progress to higher tiers.

New loyalty program

The redesigned loyalty program features three tiers: Adept of style, Master of style, and Icon of style. Each tier offers a personalized discount of up to 25%.

Personalized discounts powered by ML
A machine learning model calculates each user’s discount based on their profile and shopping preferences. For example, new users may receive a higher initial discount to encourage their first purchase, while existing users get personalized offers based on their preferences, such as their favorite brands.

Exclusive benefits at each tier
Every tier comes with additional benefits, with more opportunities unlocked as users progress. For example, users at the Master Style and Icon Style tiers can choose their favorite product category and receive a discount on it throughout the month.

Clear tier maintenance requirements
To maintain their current tier, users need to spend a certain amount and keep their purchase completion rate at or above 20%.

Project goals

  • Simplify the loyalty program rules and communicate its value more effectively.
  • Increase the number of orders placed by new customers.
  • Increase average order value (AOV).
  • Improve customer retention and encourage repeat purchases.
  • Enable more flexible management of customer discounts.

Market research for inspiration

For inspiration, I analyzed loyalty programs in other e-commerce apps. Most of them had a minimalistic design. However, our goal was to create something bright and eye-catching, so users would notice the changes

Visual identity created by the Marketing team

We received the loyalty program’s visual identity from the Marketing team. However, when applying it to the interface, I noticed that the colour used for the Iconic tier was too muted for the highest membership level.

I refined the visual identity to make it work better within the product interface and aligned the changes with the Marketing team.

I also decided not to use italic typography in the interface, as it made the overall product typography feel too visually cluttered.

Wireframes

Together with the Product Director, we defined the key information and features that should be displayed on the profile:

  • Loyalty level
  • Discount amount (without making it the main focus)
  • Progress bar with short messages to keep users informed about their progress
  • Favorite categories and the ability to choose them
  • Personalized offers (when available)

Based on these decisions, I created wireframes and presented them to the Product Director for review and approval.

  • Profile — no categories selected
  • Profile — categories selected + personalized offers
  • Loyalty program page
  • Learn more about each benefit
  • Modal prompting users to choose new categories
  • Favorite category selection
  • 2 out of 3 categories selected
  • Catalog

Loyalty program mvp

During UX testing, we identified several insights:

  • Users didn’t understand that the discount on their favourite category is added to the main discount. To make this clearer, we added a “+” sign and reinforced the message in the copy.
  • The wording “Loyalty program terms and conditions” was perceived as a legal agreement, so we changed it to “Frequently Asked Questions”.
  • Users pointed out that they couldn’t select all three categories at once, so we removed the restriction in the interface, allowing them to select three categories simultaneously.
  • Users found the new loyalty program visually appealing.

Results

The new loyalty program was initially launched to 30% of users and tested over a 2.5-month period

  • The program improved all key financial metrics.
  • New users placed their first orders faster.
  • The average number of items per order increased, without any increase in return rates.
  • No negative reviews were received on the App Store during the test period.

In September, the program was rolled out to 100% of users
